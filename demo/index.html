    What this harness reflects

    Real today

    • Unicode 18.0.0 segmentation through ICU4X: grapheme clusters, UAX #14 break opportunities, word boundaries, scripts and emoji sequences. The engine-components panel reports the engine's own versions.
    • HarfRust shaping of the engine's runs, with per-atom font fallback over the thirteen faces registered here (Latin sans, serif and mono; Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese; Devanagari, Thai, Lao, Khmer, Myanmar; Hebrew and Arabic), keyed by script and locale (SHP-12, SHP-25): a CJK run skips the Latin faces that merely share its punctuation and takes the CJK face of its locale from one fixed stack.
    • Greedy line breaking at UAX #14 opportunities with the emergency cluster break, tabs, hard breaks and blank lines.
    • The Unicode Bidirectional Algorithm (UAX #9): paragraph levels, bracket pairs, and each line reordered after breaking, so Arabic and Hebrew read right to left with embedded Latin and digits.
    • CJK autospace by profile, punctuation compression, justification through the priority ladder (word spaces, autospace, CJK gaps, letter gaps) and hanging punctuation by the chosen hang mode.
    • Strut-derived line boxes, caret stops in the output, and the output gate every paragraph passes before it is drawn.
    • Interactive hit-testing, caret geometry, cursor movement, selection geometry (Tight / Max / Strut), and IME pre-edit range_rects are wired into the canvas and text box.

    Not yet implemented

    • Vertical writing modes and annotations/ruby: rejected by the frontend. Decoration and explicit bidi spans exist in the engine but this page offers no way to mark them.
    • Kashida: Arabic lines justify at word spaces only. Hyphenation and clamping are not applied.
    • Balanced and Publication line-breaking strategies: every strategy breaks greedily today.
    • Colour glyphs (COLR, sbix, CBDT) are not painted, and no emoji face is registered here.
    • Simplified versus Traditional Han is never guessed from the characters. Untagged Han takes the locale field; inline tags in the text become the engine's style spans, which is how a rich-text host states a language switch.

    This list tracks the crate's Phase 2 scope (mutil-engine/src/middleend/layout/mod.rs and mutil-engine/src/lib.rs). If the engine gains a stage, this page should change with it, not before it.
